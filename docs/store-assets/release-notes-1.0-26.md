# Release notes — Mirrolit 1.0 (versionCode 26), closed testing

## Play Console → "What's new" (en-US)

```
Books now show all their text: chapter openings, later chapter titles, lists, verse and preformatted passages that some classic editions lost. Very large books show a clear message instead of closing the app, and re-opening a book you already own from the Catalog no longer says the library is full.
```

## Note for testers

**Mirrolit 1.0 (26) — what to try**

1. Open Project Gutenberg's illustrated "Alice's Adventures in Wonderland": each chapter starts with
   "CHAPTER I", its title and the opening paragraph ("ALICE was beginning to get very tired…"), with the
   drop-cap letter joined to its word.
2. Open "Moby-Dick", "Dracula" or "Don Quixote" from the Catalog: chapter titles inside the text and
   verse or quotations appear where they belong.
3. On the free tier with three books, open one of them again from the Catalog — it opens, no
   "Library is full".
4. Illustrated books (for example "The Count of Monte Cristo" from the Catalog) still open with their
   pictures.
5. FB2 books with pictures open as before.
