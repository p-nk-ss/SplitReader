# Release notes — Mirrolit 1.0 (versionCode 27), closed testing

## Play Console → "What's new" (en-US)

```
Tapping a chapter title now shows or hides the reading controls, just like tapping the text.
```

## Note for testers

**Mirrolit 1.0 (27) — what to try**

1. On a phone (or a tablet in portrait, original above and translation below), open a book and hide the
   controls. Tap a chapter title ("— 2 —", "Chapter 2"): the controls appear. Tap it again: they hide.
2. On a tablet side by side, the same tap still works and toggles the controls once (not on and off).
3. Scrolling by dragging from a chapter title still scrolls the book.
