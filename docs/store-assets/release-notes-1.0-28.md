# Release notes — Mirrolit 1.0 (versionCode 28), closed testing

## Play Console → "What's new" (en-US)

```
Deleting a book now keeps your place, bookmarks and notes in case you add it again (tick the box to delete them too). On phones, tapping anywhere on the page shows or hides the reading controls. Fixes: Google Drive books at the free limit, safer book files, chapter jumps.
```

## Note for testers

**Mirrolit 1.0 (28) — what to try**

1. Delete a book you were reading (leave the box unticked), then add it again from the Catalog or the
   same file: it opens where you stopped, with its bookmarks and notes. Delete it again with
   "Also delete my reading progress, bookmarks and notes" ticked: adding it again starts from the beginning.
2. A deleted book no longer counts toward the three free books.
3. On a phone (original above, translation below), hide the controls and tap empty space — between
   paragraphs, beside a short line, below the end of a chapter: the controls appear. A tap on a
   paragraph still toggles them once.
4. With three books on the free tier, open one of them again from Google Drive: it opens, no
   "Library is full".
5. Pick a chapter from the chapter list: the page lands on that chapter's title.
6. After updating, your library, covers, reading places and bookmarks are all as they were.
