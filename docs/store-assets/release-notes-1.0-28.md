# Release notes — Mirrolit 1.0 (versionCode 28), closed testing

## Play Console → "What's new" (en-US)

```
On phones, tapping anywhere on the page — even between paragraphs or below the last line — now shows or hides the reading controls.
```

## Note for testers

**Mirrolit 1.0 (28) — what to try**

1. On a phone (original above, translation below), hide the reading controls and tap empty space: between
   two paragraphs, beside a short line, below the end of a chapter — in the top and in the bottom half.
   The controls appear; tap again and they hide.
2. Tapping a paragraph still shows or hides the controls once (not on and off).
3. With a word selected, tapping empty space clears the selection first.
4. Scrolling, dragging the divider between the halves and selecting words work as before.
