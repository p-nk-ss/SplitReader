# Release notes — Mirrolit 1.0 (versionCode 7), internal testing

## Play Console → "What's new" (en-US, 136 chars)

```
Fixed: turning the phone no longer jumps to the start of the book. Bookmark jumps and switching illustrations now keep your exact place.
```

## Note for internal testers

**Mirrolit 1.0 (7) — what to try**

1. Open a book in the middle, turn the device to landscape and back: the same paragraph stays on top.
   Leave the book and reopen it: still there.
2. On a tablet, bookmark a paragraph near the end of a book, go to the start, open Bookmarks and tap it:
   it lands exactly on that paragraph, even while the translation is still loading.
3. Turn illustrations off and on in the Display dialog: you stay on the paragraph you were reading.
