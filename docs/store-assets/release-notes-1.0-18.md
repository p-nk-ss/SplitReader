# Release notes — Mirrolit 1.0 (versionCode 18), closed testing

## Play Console → "What's new" (en-US, 70 chars)

```
Clock and battery icons are readable on light themes. Android 15 ready.
```

## Note for testers

**Mirrolit 1.0 (18) — what to try**

1. On the Paper or Sepia theme, the clock, Wi-Fi and battery icons at the top are dark and easy to
   read (they were white and nearly invisible before).
2. Switch to Night or AMOLED in Settings: the icons turn light right away.
3. Everything else should look exactly as before: nothing hidden under the status bar or the
   navigation bar, with gesture navigation and with three buttons.
