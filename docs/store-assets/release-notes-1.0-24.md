# Release notes — Mirrolit 1.0 (versionCode 24), closed testing

## Play Console → "What's new" (en-US)

```
Short Chinese and Japanese passages inside other books are now translated correctly.
```

## Note for testers

**Mirrolit 1.0 (24) — what to try**

1. Open a book in English (or another language) that quotes a short sentence in Chinese or Japanese:
   the passage is now recognised and translated from its own language (or offered with
   "Download and translate"), instead of coming out as garbage.
