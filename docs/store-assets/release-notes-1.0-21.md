# Release notes — Mirrolit 1.0 (versionCode 21), closed testing

## Play Console → "What's new" (en-US)

```
Passages in another language get their own translation. Chinese and Japanese read better offline.
```

## Note for testers

**Mirrolit 1.0 (21) — what to try**

1. Open a book that quotes another language (a letter in French in an English novel, Latin
   epigraphs in Don Quixote): that passage is translated from its own language, or shown as it
   is with a small note above it ("FR · from French", "LA · Latin, not translated").
2. With Offline HQ, a passage in a language whose pack isn't downloaded shows a
   "Download and translate" button. Nothing downloads until you tap it.
3. Chinese and Japanese books with Offline HQ or ML Kit: long chapters no longer hang on
   "Preparing translation…", and Japanese sentences no longer run together ("упал.Тот").
4. Tap a word inside a passage in another language: the popup translates it from that language.
