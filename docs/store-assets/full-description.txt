Read any book in two languages at once. Mirrolit shows the original text and a live translation next to it, so you understand every line while you keep reading the real book.

Open an EPUB, FB2, or classic MOBI file. Mirrolit detects the source language and translates the book paragraph by paragraph into the language you pick, right next to the original.

WHY MIRROLIT

• Two languages, one page — on tablets and in landscape the translation sits beside the original; on a phone held upright it sits below, with a draggable divider and synchronised scrolling.
• Private by default — the built-in translators run fully on your device. Once a language is downloaded, they work offline and nothing you read leaves your phone.
• Automatic language detection — just open a book.
• 18 target languages — English, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Turkish, Swedish, Czech, and Russian.

TRANSLATION ENGINES, YOUR CHOICE

• ML Kit (on-device) — free, no setup, works offline.
• Offline HQ (on-device) — higher-quality translation with Mozilla's open translation models. Each language pair is a one-time download, then it runs offline.
• Quick Translate — fast online translation, no account needed.
• Bring your own key — DeepL, Google Cloud Translation, Microsoft Azure Translator, or LibreTranslate (including self-hosted). Keys are stored encrypted on your device.

Translations are cached on your device, so a paragraph is never translated twice.

TOOLS FOR LANGUAGE LEARNERS

• Tap a word or select a sentence for an instant translation, then save it to your vocabulary with its context.
• Bookmarks and notes as you read.
• The Almanac tracks your reading streak, minutes, a 26-week activity heatmap, and time by book and language.
• Your reading position is kept for every book.

A READER THAT FEELS LIKE A BOOK

• Editorial typography — 4 themes (Paper, Sepia, Night, AMOLED) and 12 reading typefaces.
• Fine-tune font size, line height, paragraph and letter spacing, indent, justification, and hyphenation.
• Illustrations from EPUB and FB2 books, shown inline.
• Read aloud with built-in text-to-speech, with adjustable rate and pitch.

FREE BOOKS BUILT IN

• Browse and download public-domain classics from Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks inside the app — no account, no cost.
• Import your own books from device storage or Google Drive.

PRIVACY FIRST

• No account needed to read. Sign-in is optional.
• Your books, vocabulary, and reading stats stay on your device.
• Online engines receive text only when you choose one.
