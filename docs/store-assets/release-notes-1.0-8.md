## Note for internal testers

**Mirrolit 1.0 (8) — what to try**

1. Offline HQ: switch to a language you haven't downloaded and long-press a word right away. One
   download, the same percentage in the banner and the popup, then the translation — no "corrupted" error.
2. Open a short one-chapter book: the footer starts at 0% and grows as you read; the Library card
   shows the same number and the book is under "Reading".
3. Library and Words read "1 VOLUME" / "1 saved word" when there is one.
